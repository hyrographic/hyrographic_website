  • Car Posters

These posters are hand-designed illustrations of a selection of cars such as the iconic Toyota Supra A80 and its twin-turbos. Each poster is drawn line by line by a pen plotter onto silver metallic card, with specs and feature callouts. The result is real ink lines with a subtle depth and variation which printing can't match.

Dimensions: 40 cm x 30 cm (Please message me for custom sizes and card colours)
