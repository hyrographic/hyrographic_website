The 12 Books in 12 Months bookmark is the first challenge bookmark I've created, which gives you the goal of reading 1 book each month for a year! And of course, it also works as a normal bookmark to keep track of your progress through each book.

Its simple, with each read you get the satifying reward of tearing away a tab. Watch the stack of books beneath slowly reveal itself as your reading list grows to 12 books. A great gift for new readers, or readers who need a bit of extra motivation to keep up their reading!

All bookmarks are handmade in the UK using recyclable materials.
